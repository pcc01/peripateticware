WASHINGTON STATE HOMESCHOOL ANNUAL REPORT TEMPLATE
Statute: RCW 28A.200.010

REQUIRED FIELDS for a valid Washington annual report:
- student_name (full legal name)
- parent_guardian_name (the person of record who filed the Declaration of Intent)
- reporting_period (academic year, e.g. "September 2025 through June 2026")
- subjects_covered — Washington requires instruction in: reading, writing, spelling,
  language, mathematics, social studies, science, art, music, health, PE, and
  occupational education (as applicable by grade level)
- hours_of_instruction (minimum 1,000 hours per year per RCW 28A.225.010)
- assessment_method — one of: standardized test, portfolio review,
  annual assessment by a certificated person, or approved alternative

REPORT STRUCTURE:
1. Declaration of Intent on File: [Yes / No — indicate filing date if known]
2. Student Information
3. Academic Year and Hours of Instruction
4. Subjects and Progress
5. Assessment Method Used This Year
6. Parent/Guardian Certification

CERTIFICATION LANGUAGE:
"I certify that [student_name] received home-based instruction meeting the
requirements of RCW 28A.200.010 during the reporting period stated above, and
that instruction was provided in the subjects required under Washington law."
