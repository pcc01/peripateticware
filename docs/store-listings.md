### Description  *(4000 chars max)*
```
Peripateticware turns the world outside the classroom into where learning happens. Named for Aristotle, who taught while walking with his students, the app guides learners through hands-on activities rooted in real, physical places.

A teacher or parent assigns an activity. The student walks to the site, works through a short guided sequence, and turns their field work in — all from a phone.

HOW AN ACTIVITY WORKS
• Brief — read what you'll be investigating and why it matters
• Orient — arrive on site, look around, notice what's there
• Inquiry — observe closely and capture evidence: photos, voice memos, video, and written notes, tied to the spot where you found them
• Reflect — answer open-ended prompts about what the place taught you
• Submit — send the finished field work to your teacher

SCAVENGER HUNTS & MULTI-POINT HUNTS
Some activities are location hunts. A single-point hunt sends you to one place to find and document something. A multi-point hunt is an ordered route — the app shows distance and direction to the next stop and marks it reached when you arrive. Arrival is detected on your device; no location trail is stored.

ASK PERI
A built-in assistant answers questions about the activity or what you're seeing in the field — a nudge toward the next observation, not an answer key.

BUILT FOR THE FIELD
• Works offline — activities and captures are saved on the device and sync when you're back online
• Read-aloud for prompts and questions
• Gentle reminder if you wander outside the activity area
• Adjustable themes and text

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
Location is optional and off until a teacher turns it on and a guardian signs off. On-device arrival detection records only that a stop was reached — an index and a time, never a coordinate. Richer location features (a coordinate on a photo, a live pin for a supervised trip) are separate opt-ins, each with its own retention limit. Photos, recordings, and notes belong to the student; teachers see only what's submitted. No ads. No third-party ad tracking. Data is never sold.

WHERE ACTIVITIES COME FROM
Teachers and parents build activities on the Peripateticware website (peripateticware.com). Every activity type except scavenger hunts and multi-point scavenger hunts is created there. In the app, students complete assigned activities and can propose their own for approval.

WHAT YOU NEED
A Peripateticware account. Students sign in with credentials from their teacher or school; families can start a homeschool account with a free trial. This is a companion to a class or homeschool setup, not a standalone catalogue of lessons.

Languages: English, with Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic, Hebrew, Turkish, and Canadian French.

Questions or support: <support@peripateticware.com>
```

### Age Rating
- Made for Ages: **9+** *(recommended — the four-phase inquiry flow and reflective writing suit upper-elementary and up; nothing in the content requires a higher rating).*
- Not submitted to the **Kids Category** — the app collects student work and location, includes an AI assistant, and depends on a school/guardian account, which the Kids Category's rules on data collection and external interaction don't accommodate. COPPA/FERPA compliance is handled through school authorization and guardian consent instead.
- Questionnaire: no objectionable content in any category. Unrestricted web access: **No** (Ask Peri is a bounded assistant, not a browser).

### App Privacy ("Nutrition Label") — answers
Data collected and **linked to the user**, used only for **App Functionality** (not tracking, not ads):
- **Precise Location** — only while using the app, only when an activity enables it; on-device arrival events store no coordinate.
- **Photos or Videos**, **Audio Data** — field evidence the student captures.
- **Other User Content** — written notes and reflections.
- **Name**, **Email Address** — the account, provided by the school or family.
- **Coarse Location** — geofence "activity area" checks.
- **Crash Data / Performance Data** — optional diagnostics.
Data **NOT** collected: contacts, browsing history, search history, purchases (billing is web-side via the payment processor), identifiers for advertising.
**Tracking:** None. The app does not track users across apps or websites.
